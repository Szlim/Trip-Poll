Before we fly

Crew briefing

  1. Set up Alipay and switch the city to Chongqing. Turn on the Chongqing Metro transit code to tap through the gates, and use Didi inside Alipay for rides.
  2. Use Amap (Gaode) or Baidu Maps, not Google Maps. Station exits open onto very different street levels, so check the exit before you leave the station.
  3. Book the Yangtze River Cableway 1 to 2 days ahead on the WeChat mini-program 长江索道.
  4. Skip taxis on the Yuzhong peninsula from 17:30 to 19:30. The metro is faster then.
  5. Carry passports. Hotels and bathhouses ask for them, and some venues turn away foreign IDs, so check ahead.
  6. Tell the villa about late nights. Quiet hours run 22:00 to 09:00.
